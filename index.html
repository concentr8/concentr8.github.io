The hard part is starting. We help you with that.

Concentr8 is a study and focus app for students with ADHD: pick one task, start it, and keep going with a timer, music, a body-double room and a block list for distracting apps.

Concentr8 runs on your laptop. Open this page there, or use the web app right here.

Ready in three minutes

The first app versions show the web app in a window. Calls and app blocking are being moved over step by step.

1. Install it

Open the download and drag Concentr8 into Applications. If macOS says it is damaged, run this once in Terminal:

xattr -cr /Applications/Concentr8.app

2. Open it

Your tasks, timer and settings stay on your computer.

3. Pick one task

Write it down, start the timer, and add music or a body-double room if that helps.

One task. Then follow through.

A typical evening with Concentr8. The phone call and the app blocker are the next features; this is the plan, not yet in the download.

  • 6:00

    The call

    At the time you picked: which task, and when? One sentence.

  • 9:30

    You start

    Your session begins at the time you said.

  • 9:41

    You drift

    A gentle warning, then back to your work.

  • 10:15

    You are stuck

    One minute of breathing, then one tiny step.

  • 11:00

    A break

    Take it on purpose. No nudges.

Built on research, not hunches

Every part of Concentr8 goes back to a study about getting started, several of them with people who have ADHD.

½ grade

College students with ADHD earned about half a grade less in all four years, and fewer stayed enrolled.

Why we built Concentr8.

DuPaul et al., 2021 · 406 college students
Since 1997

UC Berkeley researchers have followed girls with ADHD into adulthood. Trouble with school did not simply fade with age.

Why it checks in every day, not once.

Hinshaw lab, UC Berkeley · 228 participants
94 studies

Deciding exactly when you will start, called an if-then plan, clearly helps people follow through.

So the call asks: which task, and when?

Gollwitzer & Sheeran, 2006 · meta-analysis
If → then

If-then plans helped children with ADHD hold back unwanted responses, up to the level of children without ADHD.

The same idea, tested with ADHD.

Gawrilow & Gollwitzer, 2008
2 in 3

Adults with ADHD who learned skills like breaking big tasks into small steps: more than 2 in 3 saw symptoms drop by 30% or more, against about 1 in 3 with relaxation training.

So when you are stuck, you get one tiny first step.

Safren et al., JAMA 2010 · randomized trial
5 min

Five minutes a day of structured breathing, box breathing among them, improved mood over four weeks.

So "I'm stuck" starts with a minute of box breathing.

Balban et al., Stanford 2023 · 108 adults

These studies are why we designed Concentr8 this way. Concentr8 itself has not been tested in a study, and it is not a medical product.

Questions

macOS says the app is damaged.

It is not, it is just not signed by Apple yet. Run this once in Terminal:

xattr -cr /Applications/Concentr8.app
Windows says it protected my PC.

Click More info, then Run anyway.

How do I start the Linux version?

Make it runnable, then double-click it. Newer Ubuntu may need libfuse2.

chmod +x Concentr8-x86_64.AppImage
Do I get the newest version?

Not automatically yet. Come back to this page for the newest stable version.

Where is my data?

The app keeps your tasks and settings on your computer.
