Concentr8

Privacy policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Concentr8 is a focus app for students. This page says what the app stores, what it sends to other services, and how you delete it. If something here is unclear, write to jonas.juchelka@gmail.com.

What stays on your computer

Your tasks, messages and focus history are kept in your browser's local storage or in the Concentr8 Mac app. They are not uploaded unless a feature below says so.

What our server stores

  • If you sign in with Google: your Google account ID and your email address, so we can recognize you next time.
  • If you connect your Google Calendar: an encrypted access key (a refresh token) and which calendars you picked, at most three.
  • If you set up the morning phone call: your phone number, call time, time zone, your consent to be called, and the titles of your open tasks.

We keep this until you delete your account. We do not sell it and we do not use it for advertising.

Google Calendar

Concentr8 asks for read-only access to your Google Calendar (the scope calendar.readonly). We use it only to show your events next to your tasks and to suggest free time for focus sessions. We cannot change, add or delete your events. Events are fetched when you open the calendar and are held in your browser's memory. They are not saved on our server and they are not sent to the AI.

You can remove Concentr8's access at any time in your Google account under Security, Third-party access, or by deleting your Concentr8 account.

Concentr8's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Services that receive some of your data

  • Claude (Anthropic): when you chat with the agent, your last messages and the titles of your open tasks are sent to Claude to write an answer. The server keeps no chat history.
  • Voice providers: phone calls and spoken cues are made with voice services (currently Cartesia and ElevenLabs). They receive the text that is spoken, your phone number for calls, and, if you use voice input, your audio.
  • Microsoft Azure: our server runs there (region West US 3).
  • Google: sign-in and calendar access as described above.

Without an account you get 12 free chat messages a day. A random code made in your browser, not linked to you, counts them.

Phone calls

We call you only after you entered your number and agreed to be called. You can say "stop" on the call, pause the calls in the app, or delete your account.

Deleting your data

In the app, Profile, Data, you can export your data, reset it, or delete your account. Deleting your account removes your stored phone number, tasks, settings and Google keys from our server, and Concentr8 asks Google to revoke its access. If something does not work, email us and we delete it by hand.

Children

Concentr8 is made for university students and is not meant for children under 16.

Changes

When the app starts to store or send something new, we change this page first and update the date above.
